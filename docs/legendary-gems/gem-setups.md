# Gem Setups

> Recommended gem setups for different content types.

---

## Overview

Some Legendary Gems might work great in certain content while being totally useless in other situations. Here are some suggestions for Gem Setups for different content.

## PVP

In PVP, the best Gem Setup depends on your **class**, your **role**, the **PVP-Meta**, and sometimes the **content** (e.g., different setups for Tower / BG / Platform).

### General PVP Guidelines

**Generally good:**
- Gems that increase your movement speed
- Gems with CC (Crowd Control) Effects
- Gems that give you immunity or reduce harmful effect durations
- Anti-Meta Gems

**If your role is damage dealer:**
- Gems that increase your burst damage
- Gems that increase your Critical Hit Chance

**If your role is a tank or if you need more survivability:**
- Gems that provide shields
- Gems that reduce damage taken

**If you're running a full DoT build:**
- Gems with DoT effects (see Open World Farming section)

## Content-Specific Tips

### Tower of Victory
- Prioritize consistent damage output
- Consider defensive gems for higher floors
- Movement speed helps with positioning

### Battlegrounds
- Remember most gems are strifed (70% reduction)
- Look for gems marked "not strifed"
- CC effects are very valuable
- Anti-meta gems can counter popular builds

### Platform PVP
- Burst damage is king
- Mobility gems are crucial
- Consider the platform layout when choosing gems

### Cycle of Strife
- Balance between offense and defense
- Consider team composition
- Adapt to enemy team's build

## Build-Specific Recommendations

### Channeling Builds
- **Tear of the Comet** - Missile damage while channeling
- **Trickshot Gem** - Slower energy consumption
- **Ironbane** - Channeling triggers armorbreak

### Summoner Builds
- **Freedom & Devotion** - Longer summon duration + damage
- **Follower's Burden** - Damage per summon

### Critical Hit Builds
- **Roiling Consequence** - CHC + burn on crit
- **Mother's Lament** - CHC buff
- **Berserker's Eye** - CHC + damage
- **Blood Foe** - CHD bonus

### DoT Builds
- **Viper's Bite** - Poison DOT
- **Everlasting Torment** - Agony DOT
- **Seeping Bile** - Spreading poison
- **Roiling Consequence** - Burn on crit
- **Starfire Shard** - Ground burn

### Tank Builds
- **Battleguard** - Melee damage reduction
- **Frozen Heart** - Shield + freeze
- **Spiteful Blood** - Shield + immunity
- **Blessing of the Worthy** - Counter damage + DR
- **Phoenix Ashes** - Cheat death
