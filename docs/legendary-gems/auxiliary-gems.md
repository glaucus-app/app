# Auxiliary Gems

> Guide to using auxiliary gems for PVP builds.

---

## What are Auxiliary Gems?

If you've built up a 5-Star Legendary Gem just for gaining Resonance, or if it's a gem that is not suited for your class, your build, or PVP in general, there is the possibility to replace its effect by **slotting in an Auxiliary 5-Star Gem** on top of your existing Gem.

**Key Points:**
- You will keep your Resonance
- The Auxiliary Gem replaces the existing previous Gem Effect with its Level-1 effect

## When to Use Auxiliary Gems

### Scenario 1: Resonance-Focused Gem

You have a 5-Star gem that you upgraded primarily for Resonance (e.g., a low-tier gem you got lucky with). Its effect doesn't fit your build, but you want to keep the Resonance.

**Solution**: Slot an Auxiliary gem with an effect that benefits your build.

### Scenario 2: Wrong Gem Type

You have a defensive gem but play an offensive build, or vice versa.

**Solution**: Use an Auxiliary gem that matches your playstyle.

### Scenario 3: PVP Optimization

Some gems are great for PVE but weak in PVP due to strife or mechanics.

**Solution**: Replace the PVE-focused effect with a PVP-viable Auxiliary effect.

---

## How to Obtain Auxiliary Gems

Auxiliary gems are 5-Star gems, which can be obtained through:

1. **Legendary Crests** - Rare drop from running elder rifts
2. **Marketplace** - Purchase from other players
3. **Events** - Special event rewards
4. **Battle Pass** - Some seasons offer 5-Star gems

---

## Important Notes

- The Auxiliary gem only provides its **Level-1 effect**, regardless of the Auxiliary gem's actual rank
- You still benefit from the **Resonance** of your main gem
- The Resonance of the Auxiliary gem itself does not count
- Choose Auxiliary gems based on your build needs, not the gem's tier
