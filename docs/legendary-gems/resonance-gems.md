# Resonance Gems

> Complete guide to resonance gem combinations and effects for all legendary gems.

---

## Overview

When your Legendary Gems reach **Rank 4**, you can slot in other Legendary Gems (Resonance Gems) to unlock effects and further boost your resonance.

To unlock the effects, you have to slot in specific Legendary Gems.
