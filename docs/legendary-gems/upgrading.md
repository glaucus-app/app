## Gem Power

Each Legendary Gem can be used to upgrade another legendary gem. It becomes "fodder" for the other gem, and the upgrade material itself is "Gem Power".

> **Warning**: You can use already upgraded gems as fodder - be careful not to crunch one of your gems by accident!

## Dormant Gems (5-Star Only)

When you extract Gem Power, your 5-Star Gem becomes dormant. However, you can still socket it. It will re-awaken with all its effects when the Resonance Gems inside the Gem are filled with equal or more Gem Power than the now dormant Gem had before.

### New Resonance Calculation

The "new" Resonance of the Gem is:

1. **Original Resonance** of the Ranked Gem (e.g., 1000 for a R10 5/5)
2. **Reduced Resonance** of the Resonance Gems (e.g., 2 per Rank for a 2-Star and 10 per Rank for a 5-Star)
3. **Additional Resonance** (= Gem Power of all socketed Resonance Gems / 200). This only works with R10 5-Star Gems.

## Resonance Wings

By reaching certain Resonance Thresholds, you unlock additional Legendary Gem Sockets in your Wings, which you can use to further increase your Resonance.

> **Note**: The gems in these slots have no effects other than boosting your Resonance.

## Upgrade Cost

You can upgrade your legendary gems at the Apprentice Jeweler in Westmarch or any other safe zone (or in Castle Cyrangar).

All you need to upgrade a Legendary Gem is:
- The Gem itself
- Some Gem Power
- (Depending on the upgrade level) some more Rank 1 copies of the Legendary Gem

> **Note**: Legendary Gems can now be upgraded in smaller increments. Starting at Rank 4, Legendary Gems receive smaller incremental upgrades when combining each duplicate copy of the Gem, instead of requiring the full set to upgrade it to the next Rank. This change does not lower the overall number of Gems required to upgrade.
